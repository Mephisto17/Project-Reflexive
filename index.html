What does Lean verification establish?+

Lean checks that a formal statement follows from its recorded assumptions. The records supplied here report Lean 4.32.0 and the standard axioms propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. They are existing build evidence, not a new independent verification by this website. Matching a formal statement to a paper’s intended mathematics still calls for mathematical review.

The proof map is a curated selection of source dependencies, not a complete automatically extracted graph. Dashed arrows omit intermediate declarations. The ≈ label is shorthand for a comparison up to a universal change of density, not equality. Interactive examples illustrate ideas; they do not prove the general results.
